Anatomy of a Closed-Loop Collapse: A Causal Case Study of a Compressed VLA Policy

Poster at the IROS 2026 Workshop on Building Scalable Infrastructure for Robot Learning (ScaleInfra), Pittsburgh, October 1, 2026.

I distilled Octo-Base, a 12-layer generalist robot policy, into an 8-layer student. The student passes the offline validation checks its model family provides, then succeeds in 0 of 72 closed-loop trials of a simulated WidowX pick-and-place task, where the teacher succeeds in 40.

Four standard fixes fail under matched controls. Replacing half of each training batch with successful teacher rollouts from the deployment environment restores parity with the teacher on held-out episodes: 18 of 36 against 17 of 36.
